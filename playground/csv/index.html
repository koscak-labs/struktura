    What this does, and what it assumes

    The first part of your data (at least 192 rows, up to 30% of the file) is taken as normal. struktura calibrates its streaming monitor on it, then reads the rest one row at a time and reports where it first stops looking like that normal: a shift in level, a drift, a change in noise structure (detrended fluctuation analysis), stuck values and more. If the start of your file is already faulty, results will be misleading. It is a statistical detector: treat a flag as "look here", not a verdict.

    DFA alpha summarises how the signal's fluctuations scale: about 0.5 for uncorrelated noise, about 1.0 for 1/f noise, about 1.5 for a random walk.
